Oindrila Sinha, PhD

Postdoctoral Fellow · Georgia Institute of Technology & Emory School of Medicine

I study how perceptual learning becomes motor skill — how that relationship changes across adulthood, and how cognitive abilities shape individual differences in learning.

My research combines anticipatory gaze, motor behavior, EEG, wearable sensing, and machine learning to explain why people respond differently to the same practice — with the goal of more precise, personalized neurorehabilitation.

I earned my PhD in Motor Control (with a minor in Statistics) from Penn State and am a Jack and Dana McCallum Early Career Postdoctoral Fellow in Neurorehabilitation.
