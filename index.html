What I'm working on

One question runs through my work: why do people learn movement differently — and how can rehabilitation use that?

All research
01Perception → action

Gaze reveals learning before movement

Anticipatory eye movements expose who is learning a motor sequence — about 70 trials before reaction times improve — and reveal distinct learner profiles.

02Vision & posture

Seeing ahead to stay stable

Smooth pursuit and peripheral vision tune anticipatory hand forces, posture and long-latency reflexes before we touch a moving object.

03Wearables

Measuring prosthesis use in daily life

Embedded soft sensors and machine learning classify when an upper-limb prosthesis is actually being used for function — outside the clinic.

Why older adults learn differently
